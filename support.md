# Support

**Woodey's Place**

## Getting started

The app uses your existing iCloud account — there's no separate sign-up or login. Just install and open it; your library is created automatically and stored in your own iCloud account.

## Sharing your library

To share your library with someone else, tap the person icon on the Library screen and send them an invite. They'll need to accept it while signed into their own iCloud account on their device. Once accepted, you'll both see the same library, kept in sync automatically.

## Common issues

**Changes aren't showing up on the other person's device.**
Sync relies on iCloud. Check that both devices have an internet connection and are signed into iCloud (Settings → your name, at the top of the Settings app). It can occasionally take a minute for changes to sync in the background.

**The invite link isn't working.**
Make sure you're opening it via Mail, Messages, or another app the invite was sent through — invite links only work through the system share sheet, not if copied and pasted elsewhere.

**I want to remove someone from my shared library.**
This can be managed from the sharing screen (the same person icon on the Library screen) by the library's owner.
